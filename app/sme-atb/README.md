## Sources now used (official)
- BSEC Directive, 19 May 2026: SME trading needs a ৳30 lakh (3.00 million) portfolio of listed securities, market or cost value whichever is higher; foreign investors with a custodian account also eligible; CDBL data quarterly; QIO subscribers below the minimum may only sell. (The ৳10 lakh press figure is the QIO subscription threshold for individual Qualified Investors, not the trading threshold.)
- BSEC ATB Rules 2019 and DSE ATB Regulations 2022: 4% circuit breaker for the first two trading days, 5% from the fourth; no margin loans for equity, debt or AIF; open-end MF units sold/redeemed at asset-manager price, brokers act as agents.
- DSE site text: ATB launched 4 Jan 2023; currently traded = equity and corporate bonds; open-end MFs and AIFs are "upcoming".
